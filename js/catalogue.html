Galaxy Catalogue

Fitting code adapted from github.com/NateDeg/3KIDNAS. Single-galaxy app: Galaxy Fit.

Every WALLABY HI source in the hosted releases, ready to fit. Pick one galaxy or thousands: they go out as one DCP job of initial fits, one slice per galaxy, and as each fit lands its own bootstrap job fans out across the network. Workers fetch each galaxy's cube and mask straight from this server.

Simulation mode. Nothing is sent to DCP: jobs, timings and results below are synthetic, so you can see the flow and the visualization. Remove ?simulate from the address to deploy for real.
