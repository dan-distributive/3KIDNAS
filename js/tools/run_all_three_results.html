On the residual ~0.1–1% fortran-vs-JS/DCP gap (PA, Inc, Rₕᵢ, Vₕᵢ, etc., after the PA-conversion and noise-corner-sampling fixes): traced with matched-seed, per-realization tracing on both sides. The resampled cube, SoFiA catalogue (ellMaj/ellMin/kin_pa), geometry estimate (paEst/incEst), and noise RMS all matched to 7–9 significant figures between languages for the same realization — i.e. everything the optimizer receives as input is effectively identical. The remaining gap therefore accumulates inside the ~400+ iteration Nelder-Mead optimization itself: ordinary floating-point non-associativity between a compiled-Fortran and a JIT-compiled JS/WASM pipeline, amplified on the way out by the well-known inclination/PA degeneracy in tilted-ring fitting (which is also why RHI/VHI, which inherit that degeneracy, show the largest residual % while well-conditioned parameters like Vsys and Y stay tight). Not a discovered bug — expected cross-language floating-point noise, pending a full per-evaluation trace diff if a tighter bound is ever needed.
